About the shop

A small shop with a simple promise

I grew up in Golakganj watching my mother check every spice with her fingers and nose before it went into the kadhai. My uncle, who ran a small stall near the market, used to say that a customer who trusts your haldi will trust everything else you sell.

In 2026 I opened John’s Fresh Bites at Kachakhana to put those two lessons to work. I keep the packs small and the prices low so a family can buy only what it needs this week.

  • Every pack is 50g, so spices stay fresh and nothing sits in the tin
  • Prices from Rs 10 to Rs 35, same for everyone
  • Order by WhatsApp or phone and collect from the shop
  • You are always talking to the owner, not a call centre
  • Six spices only, so we do each one well
